# P7 — Speech language model survey

**Status:** PREPRINT (arXiv:2410.03751, 2024)  
**Cite:** W. Cui, D. Yu, X. Jiao, Z. Meng, G. Zhang, Q. Wang, Y. Guo, and I. King, “Recent Advances in Speech Language Models: A Survey,” arXiv:2410.03751, 2024.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Problem | Humans speak; most LLMs take text. Cascaded ASR+LLM+TTS loses paralinguistics, adds latency, and accumulates errors. |
| Method | Survey of cascaded ASR+LLM+TTS vs end-to-end SpeechLMs: architectures, training recipes, capabilities, evaluation metrics. |
| Dataset / environment | Survey of systems — not an Instagram cockpit. |
| Metrics | Taxonomy (latency, duplex, interruption, capability categories). Do not paste their latency numbers onto Zeref. |
| Contribution | Names the cascade Zeref already uses and the research push toward end-to-end / full-duplex speech. |
| Limitation | Speech models do not carry an operator audit row or a memory vault of confirmed corrections; tool use over a private snapshot is out of scope. |
