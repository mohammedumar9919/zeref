# P5 — Tricomi et al. Instagram engagement

**Status:** Locked  
**Cite:** P. P. Tricomi, M. Chilese, M. Conti, and A.-R. Sadeghi, “Follow Us and Become Famous! Insights and Guidelines From Instagram Engagement Mechanisms,” in *Proc. 15th ACM Web Science Conf. (WebSci ’23)*, Austin, TX, USA, 2023. DOI: 10.1145/3578503.3583623.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Problem | Popularity models often score likes only, use narrow data, and hide reasons in deep models; creators learn the score after the post exists. |
| Method | Interpretable ML on features extracted with deep models; unsupervised hot-topic finder. |
| Dataset / environment | ~10 million posts, ~34,000 influencers, nine categories, five audience tiers. |
| Metrics | Up to 94% F1 on their best category setting for high vs low engagement. **On their dataset only.** |
| Contribution | Comments matter; engagement can be partly explained. |
| Limitation | Predicts a public influencer post. Does not run an operator’s week, store that operator’s corrections, or publish under human review. |
